Purpose

These terms govern the use of the RNDS app: round timer, training journal, statistics, and sharing.

Health and safety

RNDS is a training tool, not a medical device. Heart rate and calorie measurements are indicative. Consult a doctor before resuming sport or if in doubt. Stop immediately if you feel pain, discomfort, or unusual shortness of breath.

Boxing and combat sports carry risks: train with appropriate equipment and under supervision.

Minimum age

RNDS is for people aged 15 and over. Between 13 and 15, you need the consent of a parent or legal guardian to create an account and post in the feed. By ticking the box when you sign up, you confirm that you meet this requirement.

Use

You agree to use RNDS fairly, not to post illegal content through the sharing features, and to respect the other members of your gym.

Community guidelines

Zero tolerance for hateful content, violent content outside a sports context, sexual content, harassment, impersonation, and spam. Any post, comment, or profile can be reported; the RNDS team reviews reports within 24 hours, removes the content in question, and may delete the author’s account. You can block an athlete at any time.

Liability

The publisher does everything possible to ensure the app runs properly, but can’t guarantee it will be error-free. The publisher can’t be held liable for any injury sustained during training or for the loss of unsaved data.

Changes

These terms may change. The current version is always available in the app and on this page.

Governing law

These terms are governed by French law. In the event of a dispute, an amicable resolution will be sought before any legal action. You can use a consumer mediator free of charge.
