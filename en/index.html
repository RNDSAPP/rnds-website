Custom

  • Beeps and voice

    The timer sounds at the start and end of every round, and warns you ten seconds before the end.

  • Rhythm mode

    During the round, a voice calls out the combos and the screen shows the defense to work on.

  • Your music keeps playing

    Apple Music or your podcast doesn’t stop. The volume can dip for a beep.

  • Lock screen

    The round stays on screen in a Live Activity and in the Dynamic Island.

Programs

Each program runs three to six weeks, with one focus for each session. A session counts as done once you’ve finished three quarters of its rounds.

Around your training

Prep

Sleep, meals, water and weight, in the same place as your sessions.

For weight, pick your weight class (16, from strawweight to cruiserweight) and your weigh-in date, then track your weight all the way to fight night.

Journal

Every session is logged and sorted by week and by type. Add your training partners and challenge each other.

Apple Watch
and Health

    Start a session or a run with your voice.

Do I need to create an account?

Yes, in 30 seconds: your email (you get a 6-digit code, no password) or “Continue with Apple.” That’s how you get your account back if you change iPhones.

Does RNDS work without an Apple Watch?

Yes, everything works with just your iPhone. To see your heart rate during a session, you need an Apple Watch or a Bluetooth heart rate strap.

Does the timer keep beeping with the screen locked?

Yes. Beeps and voice keep going when the screen is locked, and the round stays on screen in a Live Activity.

Where is my data?

Your journal stays on your iPhone and in Health. Only what the social features need is online. It’s all explained in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Data › Delete my account. Your online data is erased along with it.
