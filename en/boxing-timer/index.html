How many rounds, and how long?

It depends on the sport and the level. An amateur boxing bout is 3 rounds of 3 minutes, with 1 minute of rest. A men’s pro title fight goes up to 12 rounds of 3 minutes. MMA is 3 rounds of 5 minutes, and 5 rounds for title fights.

Juniors and beginners often box shorter rounds, from 90 seconds to 2 minutes. The exact format depends on age, category and governing body, so check the rules of your event.

Which format for training?

On the bag, 6 rounds of 3 minutes with 1 minute of rest make a solid 23-minute session. For a fight camp, match the rounds of fight night, then add one or two. In Muay Thai, keep the 2-minute rest of the pro format: that is the recovery you will get in the fight.

BJJ has no rounds in competition: a match lasts 5 to 10 minutes depending on the belt. In training, people chain 5 or 6-minute rolls with a minute to switch partners.

Frequently asked questions

Is the timer free?

Yes. The RNDS online boxing timer is free, with no account and no ads. It runs entirely in your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Why can’t I hear the bell on iPhone?

On iPhone, silent mode mutes web pages. Turn it off, raise the volume and press Start: that tap is what allows sound. If the screen locks, the browser stops ringing.

Can I show it on the gym TV?

Yes. Open the page on a computer connected to the TV and press Full screen (or the F key): the numbers fill the screen and can be read from the ring. From an iPhone, use screen mirroring.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, yes: everything runs on your device. Keep the tab open.

How is it different from the RNDS app?

The app rings even with the screen locked, without stopping your music, and keeps every round in your journal. It adds a rhythm mode, programs and Apple Watch support. It is free on the App Store.
